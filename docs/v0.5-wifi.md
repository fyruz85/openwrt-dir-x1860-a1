Include upstream MT7915 firmware and require its PCIe driver in the resolved build configuration. This is a compile-only experiment: NAND and PCIe remain disabled and no radio is expected to enumerate. No factory, recovery or sysupgrade image is permitted.

References: https://openwrt.org/inbox/toh/d-link/dir-x1860_a1 and upstream commit 13dc286814c646a2f03dc27e5bf2a34206e0242d (B1 package selection only). The A1 GPL archive remains unavailable; https://tsd.dlink.com.tw/GPL/ lists the source request address GPLCODE@dlink.com. The A1 boot menu offers RAM loading but Ethernet-only invocation is unverified. Relative EEPROM/precal clues are not verified absolute flash addresses. Do not reuse B1 partition, MAC or PCIe wiring assumptions. Driver compilation does not establish radio functionality. No device access or flash operations authorized.
